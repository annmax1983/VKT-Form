VKT Form is a form snapshot and auto-fill extension for Chrome and Edge. Fill a long form once, save every field as a snapshot, and put it back into the page with one click the next time you need it. It works on ordinary forms and on the modern React, Vue and Angular forms that ordinary autofill often leaves half empty. Snapshots, fills, field calibration and JSON backup are free and unlimited; there is no account and no cloud. Premium optionally syncs snapshots between your devices through your browser account.

Everything it does

The core features at a glance.

01

Collects every field of a form into one named snapshot: text, email, phone, address, dates, dropdowns, checkboxes, radio buttons and rich text.

02

Fills complex React, Vue and Angular forms that ignore ordinary autofill, because values are written the way those components expect.

03

Reaches fields inside Shadow DOM, iframes and rich-text editors, and also captures the inactive steps of a multi-step form.

04

Verifies every field after writing and reports the ones it could not match, so an auto-fill never silently misses half the form.

05

Field calibration: bind a stubborn field to the exact box on the page once, and it fills correctly from then on.

06

Survives redesigns: matching works from field names, labels, placeholders and semantic meaning, not from screen coordinates.

Also included

  • Unlimited snapshots and unlimited fills, free, with no account.
  • Export and import all snapshots as one JSON file for backup or for moving to another computer.
  • Snapshots are tied to a normalised page address, so similar pages keep separate snapshots and long query strings do not get in the way.
  • Everything runs in a side panel: collect, fill, update, calibrate and organise snapshots without leaving the page.
  • Six languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, German, Spanish and French, following your browser language.
  • Optional Premium sync keeps snapshots in step between computers through your Chrome or Edge account — no sync server of ours.

02

Free vs Premium

The free tier is a complete tool, not a trial. Premium is optional.

Free

unlimited snapshots, unlimited fills, deep field detection (Shadow DOM, iframes, rich text, multi-step forms), read-back verification, field calibration, snapshot update, JSON export and import, six languages and the full side-panel workflow. There is no snapshot cap and no daily fill cap.

Premium

cross-device sync through your Chrome or Edge account, so the same snapshots are available on every computer signed into the same browser, plus priority support. One licence key activates on two browsers, and removing the licence from one browser frees that slot again.

The licence is optional. Everything except cross-device sync and priority support is free. Premium keeps working offline and asks the licence server for a check about every two hours; if the server cannot be reached, you are not locked out of the extension.

Private by design

Your data stays on your device. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Read exactly what is stored and when the extension uses the network.
